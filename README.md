# Telegram Bot Connector

An unofficial connector primarily designed for dots. It lets your dot interact
with you through your own Telegram bot, with a small bridge hosted in your
ChatGPT Sites account.

Copy this into your dot:

> Review https://github.com/tzuhsiao/chatgpt-telegram-bot-connector for safety. If it
> looks suitable, follow AGENTS.md to install it for me.

Details for dots and agents: [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
